Spin Wheel

Your list stays on your device

Spin Wheel picks one item from a list you provide. It runs in your browser, on your computer. There is no server, no account, and no network request from the extension.

What stays on your device

Everything the extension touches stays local:

  • Your list — names, numbers, or any lines you paste, load from a .csv or .txt file, or read from the current page when you click From selection. It is processed in the popup and saved only in the browser’s local storage so the list is still there next time you open it.
  • Weights, Time, Sound, and Remove winner — these settings stay in the same local storage. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.
  • The winner — chosen on your device before the wheel animation starts. It is shown in the popup and is not sent anywhere.

From selection reads the text you highlighted on the current tab, and only after you click that button. The extension does not watch pages in the background.

What never leaves

Spin Wheel has no backend. It does not call a server. It does not send your list, your settings, or any identifier. Permissions are exactly storage, activeTab, and scripting. There are no host permissions.

Your choices

Clear the list in the popup, or uninstall Spin Wheel. Either way the local copy is gone. We cannot recover it, because we never received it.

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis is your consent and performing the feature you asked for — picking from the list you gave. You withdraw that by not using the extension or by uninstalling it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port personal data, to object, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority (for example the ICO). In California you also have the right to know, access, delete, and correct, and not to be discriminated against for using those rights. We do not sell or share personal information. Because we hold no copy of your list, most of these rights are already met. Email us if you have a request.

Children

Spin Wheel is for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

What we do not do

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No selling or sharing of data — we never see your list.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
